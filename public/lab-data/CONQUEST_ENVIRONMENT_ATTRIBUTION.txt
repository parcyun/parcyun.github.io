Natural environment classification for GeoWeb Battleground
RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017, Dinerstein et al. (2017), CC BY 4.0.
https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014
https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/RESOLVE_ECOREGIONS_2017
Downloaded via Esri-hosted Resolve_Ecoregions feature service (847 records including rock and ice).
Source geometry simplified to 0.08 degrees; rasterized at 0.1 degrees. Tile centers sampled.
Natural Earth representative physical landforms (Public Domain) take precedence for mountains, plateaus and plains; existing GeoWeb climate highland/polar supplement fills gaps.
These are regional ecological environments, not current land cover or measured permafrost. Rock/ice is grouped as a frozen environment for gameplay, not a soil permafrost diagnosis.
Attack/defense percentages are designed game balance, not scientific measurements.
