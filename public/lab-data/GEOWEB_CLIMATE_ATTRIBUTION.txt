GeoWeb climate texture attribution

Climate groups
- Beck et al. (2023), High-resolution (1 km) Köppen-Geiger maps for 1901-2099 based on constrained CMIP6 projections
- Historical period used: 1991-2020; 0.1-degree product
- https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02549-6
- Dataset: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.21789074
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)

Highland supplement
- USGS Global Mountains K3, https://doi.org/10.5066/P9O035QJ, Public Domain
- NOAA ETOPO 2022, https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74
- Classroom rule: K3 mountain terrain at 1,500 m or higher; polar latitudes remain polar.

Processing
- Original Köppen-Geiger classes 1-30 were regrouped into A tropical, B dry,
  C temperate, D cold, and E polar. The highland supplement was then overlaid.
- Generated by scripts/build-geoweb-climate.py for elementary-level learning.
