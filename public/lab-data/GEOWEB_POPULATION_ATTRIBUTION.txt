GeoWeb population cartogram, generated 2026-11-19.
Population: 2023 observations; UN World Population Prospects 2024, processed by Our World in Data.
https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/population
https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/population.metadata.json
Boundary geometry: existing GeoWeb world.json, based on Natural Earth public-domain country boundaries.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Method reference: Gastner, M., Seguy, V., & More, P. (2018). Fast flow-based algorithm for creating density-equalizing map projections. PNAS USA, 115:E2156–E2164.
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1712674115
Implementation: go-cart-wasm@0.5.0; offline generation only. Upstream perimeter minimum-area option disabled by a checksum-guarded one-byte change equivalent to original C -n.
https://github.com/riatelab/go-cart-wasm
Known limitations: small-area convergence errors; unknown populations are null, not zero; exact proportionality belongs to the equal-area calculation plane, not all reprojected screen views.
