Adaptation: numeric 2020 gridded density (people/km²) exported to a 4096×2048 EPSG:4326 display texture; five red/opacity classes chosen for GeoWeb. NoData is transparent. Output is resampled from native 30-arc-second data and does not retain every 1km detail.
Country boundaries and geographic positions must remain unchanged. This is not national-average density or density computed from cartogram area. Model estimates are not direct observation of every cell.
