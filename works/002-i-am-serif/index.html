The Studio

I am Serif is a small studio that believes the most modern thing you can do is leave things out.

We work with a handful of clients a year — architects, publishers, and houses who value the long read over the loud one. No trends, no noise: just type set with intent, paper chosen with care, and white space treated as a material.

The result is work that ages slowly and says exactly what it means.
